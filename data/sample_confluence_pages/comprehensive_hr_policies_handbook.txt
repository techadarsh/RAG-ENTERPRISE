## 1. Employment Policies

### 1.1 Equal Employment Opportunity

**Policy Statement**:
Our company is an Equal Opportunity Employer. We are committed to creating a diverse and inclusive workplace where all employees are treated with dignity and respect, regardless of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, gender identity, sexual orientation, veteran status, or any other protected characteristic.

**Application**:
- All employment decisions (hiring, promotion, termination, compensation) are based solely on job-related criteria
- We prohibit discrimination and harassment in all forms
- Reasonable accommodations provided for qualified individuals with disabilities
- Affirmative action plan in place for underrepresented groups

**Reporting Violations**:
- Report to HR immediately (hr@company.com)
- Anonymous hotline available: 1-800-REPORT-IT
- No retaliation against reporters
- Investigations completed within 30 days

### 1.2 Employment Categories

**Full-Time Employees**:
- Work 40 hours per week
- Eligible for all company benefits
- Health insurance, 401(k) matching, PTO
- Minimum commitment: 1 year

**Part-Time Employees**:
- Work less than 30 hours per week
- Prorated PTO based on hours worked
- No health insurance or 401(k) matching
- Hourly pay, no bonuses

**Contract Employees**:
- Fixed-term engagements (3-12 months)
- Hourly or project-based pay
- No benefits package
- Can convert to full-time after 6 months

**Interns**:
- Paid internships only (minimum wage + 20%)
- 10-20 hours per week during school, 40 hours during summer
- Mentorship program included
- Potential for full-time offer after graduation

### 1.3 Background Checks

**Pre-Employment Screening**:
All final candidates undergo background checks including:
- Criminal history check (federal and state)
- Employment verification (last 3 employers)
- Education verification (degrees and certifications)
- Credit check (for finance positions only)
- Reference checks (minimum 2 professional references)

**Timeline**: 5-7 business days
**Conditional Offer**: Job offer contingent on satisfactory background check
**Adverse Action**: Candidates notified before disqualification

### 1.4 Onboarding Process

**Week 1: Orientation**

*Day 1*:
- 9:00 AM: Welcome meeting with HR
- 9:30 AM: IT setup (laptop, accounts, email)
- 10:30 AM: Company overview presentation
- 11:30 AM: Benefits enrollment
- 1:00 PM: Lunch with team
- 2:00 PM: Office tour & introductions
- 3:00 PM: Review employee handbook
- 4:00 PM: Complete I-9, W-4 forms

*Day 2-3*:
- Shadow team members
- Setup development environment
- Review codebase and documentation
- Attend team standup meetings
- Meet with manager for 90-day goals

*Day 4-5*:
- Assign first task (beginner-friendly)
- Pair programming with mentor
- Access to Slack, GitHub, Jira
- Set up direct deposit

**Week 2-4**:
- Complete onboarding checklist
- Attend department meetings
- Begin contributing to projects
- Weekly 1-on-1 with manager
- Feedback session at end of week 4

### 1.5 Probationary Period

**Duration**: 90 days for all new hires

**Evaluation Criteria**:
- Technical skills & job performance
- Team collaboration & communication
- Attendance & punctuality
- Cultural fit & company values alignment
- Initiative & problem-solving

**Reviews**:
- 30-day check-in (informal)
- 60-day review (formal written evaluation)
- 90-day final review (promotion to regular employee or termination)

**Expectations**:
- Complete all training modules
- Achieve 80%+ of 90-day goals
- No more than 2 unexcused absences
- Positive peer feedback

## 3. Time Off & Leave Policies

### 3.1 Paid Time Off (PTO)

**Accrual Rates**:

```
Years of Service    Annual PTO Days
0-2 years           15 days
3-4 years           20 days
5-7 years           25 days
8+ years            30 days

Plus:
- 10 company holidays
- 5 sick days (separate)
- Birthday off
Total: 31-46 days off per year
```

**Accrual Method**:
- PTO accrues each pay period (bi-weekly)
- Pro-rated for part-time employees
- Example: 20 days/year = 0.77 days per pay period

**Usage Rules**:
- Minimum request: 4 hours (half-day)
- Request via HR system (Workday) at least 2 weeks in advance
- Manager approval required
- Blackout periods: None (but coordinate with team)
- Carryover: Up to 5 days to next year (use-it-or-lose-it after that)
- Cash out: Not permitted

**PTO Payout on Termination**:
- Voluntary resignation: Unused PTO paid out
- Termination for cause: Forfeit unused PTO
- Layoff/involuntary: Unused PTO paid out

### 3.2 Sick Leave

**Allowance**: 5 days per year (separate from PTO)

**Usage**:
- Personal illness or injury
- Medical appointments
- Caring for sick family member
- Mental health days
- No doctor's note required (unless >3 consecutive days)

**Accrual**: Does NOT carry over to next year

**Notice**: Call manager & HR before 9:00 AM if using sick day

### 3.3 Company Holidays

**Fixed Holidays** (10 days):
1. New Year's Day (January 1)
2. Martin Luther King Jr. Day (3rd Monday in January)
3. Presidents' Day (3rd Monday in February)
4. Memorial Day (Last Monday in May)
5. Independence Day (July 4)
6. Labor Day (1st Monday in September)
7. Thanksgiving Day (4th Thursday in November)
8. Day after Thanksgiving
9. Christmas Eve (December 24)
10. Christmas Day (December 25)

**Floating Holiday**: 1 day (employee choice, manager approval)

**Holiday Pay**:
- If holiday falls on weekend, observed on adjacent weekday
- If required to work holiday (rare): Double-time pay + comp day

### 3.4 Parental Leave

**Maternity Leave** (Birth Mother):
- 16 weeks paid leave (100% salary)
- 4 weeks before due date (if desired)
- 12 weeks after birth
- FMLA protection (job guaranteed)
- Can extend with PTO or unpaid leave

**Paternity Leave** (Non-Birth Parent):
- 8 weeks paid leave (100% salary)
- Must be taken within 6 months of birth/adoption
- Can be taken intermittently or all at once

**Adoption Leave**:
- Same as paternity leave (8 weeks paid)
- Reimbursement for adoption expenses: Up to $10,000

**Return-to-Work**:
- Flexible schedule first 3 months (work from home more)
- Lactation rooms available (private, refrigerator)
- Gradual ramp-up encouraged

### 3.5 Bereavement Leave

**Immediate Family** (parent, spouse, child, sibling):
- 5 days paid leave
- Travel time included (if >200 miles)

**Extended Family** (grandparent, in-law, aunt, uncle):
- 3 days paid leave

**Additional Leave**:
- Can use PTO for more time
- Unpaid leave available if needed
- Mental health resources available (EAP)

### 3.6 Jury Duty & Voting Leave

**Jury Duty**:
- Paid leave for duration of service
- Must provide court summons to HR
- Return to work if dismissed early
- Company pays difference if jury pay < salary

**Voting**:
- 2 hours paid leave on election day (federal, state, local)
- Can arrive late or leave early

### 3.7 Sabbatical Leave

**Eligibility**: After 7 years of continuous employment

**Duration**: 4 weeks (paid)

**Purpose**:
- Travel & personal growth
- Volunteer work
- Pursue passion project
- Recharge & prevent burnout

**Requirements**:
- Submit proposal 6 months in advance
- Manager approval required
- Document learnings upon return (optional presentation)

**Frequency**: Every 7 years

- Engineers: 1 week every 8 weeks

**Lunch Break**:
- Minimum 30 minutes (unpaid)
- Recommended 60 minutes
- Off-site lunches allowed

**Rest Breaks**:
- 15 minutes every 4 hours (paid)
- Coffee breaks encouraged
- Walking meetings counted as work time

**Nursing Mothers**:
- Reasonable break time for lactation
- Private room provided (not bathroom)

## 7. Performance Management

### 7.1 Performance Review Cycle

**Annual Review** (January):
- Self-assessment
- Manager review
- 360 feedback from peers
- Goal setting for next year
- Compensation adjustments

**Mid-Year Check-In** (July):
- Progress on goals
- Course correction if needed
- Informal feedback

**Continuous Feedback**:
- Weekly 1-on-1s with manager
- Monthly team feedback sessions
- Real-time feedback encouraged (positive & constructive)

### 7.2 Performance Ratings

**Rating Scale**:

```
5 - Exceeds Expectations (Top 10%)
- Consistently delivers exceptional results
- Goes above and beyond job requirements
- Major impact on team/company success
- Bonus: 20-30% of base

4 - Meets Expectations (60-70%)
- Consistently meets all job requirements
- Reliable and high-quality work
- Positive team contributor
- Bonus: 10-15% of base

3 - Needs Improvement (10-15%)
- Meets some but not all expectations
- Inconsistent performance
- Requires manager intervention
- Bonus: 0-5% of base

2 - Does Not Meet Expectations (5-10%)
- Fails to meet minimum job requirements
- Performance improvement plan required
- At risk of termination
- No bonus

1 - Unacceptable (Rare)
- Severe performance issues
- Immediate termination considered
- No bonus
```

### 7.3 Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

**Trigger**: Performance rating of 2 or 3

**Duration**: 60-90 days

**Process**:
1. Meet with manager to discuss issues
2. Create written improvement plan with specific goals
3. Weekly check-ins with manager
4. HR oversight
5. Final review at end of period

**Outcomes**:
- Success: Return to regular status, rating improved
- Partial Success: Extend PIP another 30-60 days
- Failure: Termination

**Support During PIP**:
- Additional training provided
- Mentorship assigned
- Resources allocated
- No shame/stigma (confidential)

## 8. Workplace Conduct

### 8.1 Code of Conduct

**Core Values**:
- **Respect**: Treat everyone with dignity
- **Integrity**: Be honest and ethical
- **Collaboration**: Work together effectively
- **Excellence**: Strive for high quality
- **Innovation**: Embrace new ideas

**Expected Behaviors**:
- Communicate professionally
- Be punctual for meetings
- Meet commitments and deadlines
- Give and receive feedback gracefully
- Maintain positive attitude

**Prohibited Behaviors**:
- Harassment or discrimination
- Violence or threats
- Theft or property damage
- Drug/alcohol use at work (except company events)
- Conflicts of interest

### 8.2 Anti-Harassment Policy

**Definition of Harassment**:
Any unwelcome conduct based on protected characteristics (race, gender, religion, etc.) that creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive work environment.

**Examples of Harassment**:
- Offensive jokes or comments
- Unwanted physical contact or advances
- Display of offensive images or materials
- Retaliation against complainants
- Cyberbullying or online harassment

**Zero Tolerance**:
- First offense: Warning & mandatory training
- Second offense: Suspension
- Third offense: Termination
- Severe cases: Immediate termination

**Reporting**:
- Report to HR, manager, or anonymous hotline
- Investigation within 10 business days
- Confidentiality maintained
- No retaliation guaranteed

### 8.3 Dress Code

**General Guidelines**:
- Business casual
- Clean and professional appearance
- No offensive graphics or messages

**Examples**:
- ✅ Acceptable: Jeans, t-shirts (plain or branded), sneakers, hoodies
- ✅ Acceptable: Polo shirts, chinos, blouses, dress shoes
- ❌ Not Acceptable: Shorts, flip-flops, tank tops, revealing clothing
- ❌ Not Acceptable: Pajamas, sweatpants, clothing with holes

**Exceptions**:
- Casual Friday: More relaxed (shorts & flip-flops okay)
- Client meetings: Business professional required
- Remote work: No dress code (but professional for video calls)

## 12. Grievance & Complaint Procedures

### 12.1 Informal Resolution

**First Step**: Speak directly with person involved
- Many issues resolved through direct communication
- Be respectful and professional
- Focus on specific behaviors, not personalities

**Second Step**: Involve manager
- If direct approach fails, escalate to manager
- Manager facilitates conversation
- Document discussion

### 12.2 Formal Complaint

**When to File**:
- Informal resolution unsuccessful
- Serious violations (harassment, discrimination)
- Legal concerns

**Process**:
1. Submit written complaint to HR (hr@company.com)
2. Include: Names, dates, detailed description, witnesses
3. HR acknowledges within 2 business days
4. Investigation begins within 5 business days
5. Decision within 30 days

**Investigation**:
- Confidential (only those who need to know)
- Both parties interviewed
- Witnesses interviewed
- Evidence reviewed
- Findings documented

**Outcomes**:
- Substantiated: Disciplinary action taken (warning to termination)
- Unsubstantiated: No action, may recommend mediation
- Inconclusive: Insufficient evidence, monitoring continues

### 12.3 Appeals Process

**Right to Appeal**:
If dissatisfied with investigation outcome, employee may appeal within 10 business days.

**Appeal Process**:
1. Submit written appeal to VP of HR
2. Include: Why decision was wrong, new evidence
3. VP reviews case independently
4. Decision within 15 business days
5. VP decision is final

### 12.4 Non-Retaliation

**Policy**:
Company strictly prohibits retaliation against employees who:
- Report violations in good faith
- Participate in investigations
- Exercise legal rights

**Retaliation Examples**:
- Demotion or termination
- Reduced pay or hours
- Negative performance reviews
- Social exclusion or hostility

**Consequences**:
Retaliation is grounds for immediate termination, regardless of position.
